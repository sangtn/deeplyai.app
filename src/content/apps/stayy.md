## Rules that depend on a number

Digital nomads, expats and remote workers often have to know exactly how long they have been somewhere. Stayy detects the countries you visit and shows days spent in each on one dashboard.

## What it watches

- The 183-day tax residency rule, along with custom residency requirements.
- Schengen 90/180 usage.
- Visa validity periods.

Rules differ by country and situation. Use Stayy to stay organized, and confirm your position with the relevant authority or a tax professional.
