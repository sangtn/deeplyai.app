Get started in a minute

ROAMO is a real turn-by-turn navigation app. There is no account to create: open it and go.

  1. Allow location so ROAMO can show where you are and guide you.
  2. Search or speak: type an address or a place, tap the microphone, or pick a Nearby category such as gas, charging or coffee.
  3. Preview the route by car, bike or on foot, and compare the fastest and the shortest.
  4. Tap Go. Big turn cards, your speed next to the limit and voice guidance take you there.

22 map worlds, 2 of them free

Tap Switch world on the map and everything is redrawn: ground, water, roads, labels and your route color. Block World and Doodle Notebook are free forever; ROAMO Pro unlocks all 22.

  • Pick a day world and a night world: dark worlds such as Neon Night glow after sunset.
  • Change world in the middle of a trip, on iPhone or on CarPlay.
  • Each world can speak with its own co-pilot. Choose a voice in Settings › Co-pilot voice: Classic is free, the character voices come with Pro.

ROAMO on CarPlay

Your game-style map, search and turn-by-turn guidance on the car screen, in sync with your iPhone.

  1. Connect your iPhone to the car with a cable or wirelessly.
  2. Tap ROAMO on the CarPlay Home Screen.
  3. Search with the car keyboard or pick a recent or favorite place, then tap Go.
  4. Tap the globe button to switch world while you drive, and End to stop.

Don't see ROAMO in the car? On your iPhone open Settings › General › CarPlay, choose your car, tap Customize and add ROAMO.

On your Lock Screen and Apple Watch

While you navigate, the next turn follows you outside the app.

  • Live Activity: the next turn and your arrival time on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island. Allow it in iPhone Settings › ROAMO › Live Activities.
  • Widgets: add ROAMO to the Home Screen to start a route home or to work with one tap.
  • Apple Watch: turn cards, the map, Home, Work and your favorites on your wrist. Install it from the Watch app on your iPhone, under Available Apps.
  • Siri: say “Navigate home with ROAMO” or “Find coffee nearby with ROAMO”.

Make it yours

  • World Studio (Pro): start from any of the 22 worlds and change colors, use your photos as textures, and draw your own place icons. Tap Switch world, then Create your own world.
  • Pointer: pick a game pointer for your car, or make your own from a photo.
  • App icon: six alternate icons unlock as you drive, at 10, 50, 100, 250, 500 and 1,000 km. See Settings › App icon.
  • Share a world: send the .roamoworld file by AirDrop, Messages or Files. It never goes through a server.

Turn your trips into videos

Each trip you navigate can become a replay of your route in your world, ready for TikTok, Reels or Shorts.

  1. Trips are recorded while you navigate and stay on your iPhone. You can turn this off in Settings › Record trips.
  2. When you arrive, tap Make trip video, or open Settings › Trips & videos later.
  3. Choose a format (9:16, 1:1, 4:5 or 16:9), a length, a filter, titles and music.
  4. Save it to Photos or share it. With Pro, videos have no watermark and can use your own music.

You can also add a short clip from the front camera, without sound. It is recorded only if you turn it on, and it stays on your iPhone.

Frequently asked questions

How do I restore my purchase?
Open ROAMO, tap the gear button to open Settings, then tap Restore purchases. Make sure you are signed in to the App Store with the same Apple ID you used to buy ROAMO Pro.
How do I cancel my subscription?
Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone open Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions › ROAMO and tap Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid being charged for the next period. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.
What can I do for free?
Block World and Doodle Notebook are free forever, with search, nearby places and turn-by-turn navigation. ROAMO Pro unlocks the other 20 worlds.
Why do I see “Daily map service limit reached”?
Search and routing use a shared map service with a daily request limit. If it is reached, try again later; the limit resets every day. The map itself and your saved places keep working.
How do I change the language?
Open ROAMO, tap the gear button, and choose a language under Language. “System default” follows your iPhone's language. Some text may update after restarting ROAMO.
How do I switch between miles and kilometers, or mute the voice?
In ROAMO Settings, under Navigation, choose Units and Voice (voice, chimes or muted). You can also hide the speedometer there.
The blue dot does not move or shows the wrong place.
Check that location access is allowed in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Location Services › ROAMO, preferably with Precise Location on. Moving to an open area helps GPS lock on.
Which devices does ROAMO work on?
iPhone and iPad with iOS 17 or later, CarPlay, and Apple Watch with watchOS 10 or later. A single purchase of ROAMO Pro covers all your devices with the same Apple ID.
Are my trips and videos uploaded anywhere?
No. Trips, trip videos, the worlds you make and your saved places stay on your device. ROAMO has no account and no server that receives them.
