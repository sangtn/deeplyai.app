## Listen to the web

Paste a link or share one from Safari. WristNet strips the ads and clutter, then reads the article aloud on your Watch.

- A continuous queue moves from one article to the next, like a playlist.
- Control playback from your wrist or AirPods, at 1×, 1.25×, 1.5× or 2×.
- A sleep timer lets you drift off without draining the battery.
- Prefer to read? Open the clean, ad-free text view.

It suits commutes, workouts, walks and chores, any time your hands and eyes are busy.

## Watch your videos

Send videos from Files or Photos on your iPhone to your Apple Watch with one tap, then play them offline through the Watch speaker or Bluetooth headphones.

## Quietly private

No account is required, WristNet does not sell your data, and its article cleaning runs on your device.
