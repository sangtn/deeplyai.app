Vibe Reset

Short daily rituals in place of long to-do lists.

Requires iOS 17.6+Version 1.0, updated March 27, 2026

Get it free on the App Store

Small enough to actually do

A Vibe Pack bundles three to five tiny habits around one mood, such as Monday Morning Energy or Late Night Wind Down. Each takes 10 to 30 seconds, so a whole pack is about two minutes.

Make your own

Start with three default packs, or create custom ones with your own habits, emoji and colors, set for morning, afternoon, evening or anytime.

A satisfying finish

Each tap has a haptic click and its own small sound, with confetti when a pack is complete. No guilt if you skip a day.
