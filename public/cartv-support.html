Add your own playlist or server
  1. Tap the blue + at the top of any tab (Home, Live TV, Library, Saved).
  2. Choose the type: an M3U/M3U8 link, a file, a QR code, Xtream Codes, Plex, Jellyfin, Emby or a WebDAV share.
  3. Paste the link your provider gave you (and an XMLTV guide link if you have one), then tap Test & add.
Use Car.TV in your car

Car.TV is a CarPlay video app. It appears on the car screen in cars whose CarPlay supports video, with iOS 26.4 or later.

  1. Connect your iPhone to the car with a cable, or wirelessly if your car supports it, and allow CarPlay when your iPhone asks.
  2. On the car screen, open Car.TV.
  3. Browse Home, Live TV, Movies and Series and choose something to play. Videos you added from Files or Photos are at the top of Home, under My videos.
  4. When the car is parked, the video plays on the car display. When the car moves, CarPlay takes the picture away and the sound keeps playing, with Now Playing and the steering-wheel buttons. The video comes back when you park.

If Car.TV is not on your car screen, your car’s CarPlay may not support video yet. Car Mode and casting still work on your iPhone.

Cast to a TV

In the player, tap the cast button at the top right (it appears as soon as a TV is found on your Wi-Fi). Car.TV lists AirPlay devices, Chromecast and Google TV, and DLNA smart TVs. If none appear, check that the TV is on and on the same Wi-Fi, and that Local Network is allowed for Car.TV in Settings.

A channel won’t play
  • Test the same link in another player. If it fails there too, the provider is down or the link has expired.
  • Some formats (for example MKV with DTS audio) can’t be decoded by iOS. Car.TV marks them “Phone only” or says so instead of showing a black screen.
  • Settings › Diagnostics shows what the last attempt returned. Include it when you write to us.
Restore, cancel or get a refund for Premium
  • Restore: Settings › Restore purchases, or Restore on the paywall.
  • Cancel: Settings › Manage subscription, or in your Apple Account settings, at least 24 hours before the period ends.
  • Refund: use reportaproblem.apple.com. Apple handles all payments.
Does Car.TV include channels?

No. Car.TV is a player: it does not provide, host, sell or recommend any channels, films or series. You add your own sources and are responsible for having the right to watch them. The sample playlist holds only stock footage that we host ourselves.

What happens to my data?

Car.TV collects no data. Your playlists, favourites and watch history stay on your devices; passwords and tokens stay in the iOS Keychain. iCloud sync (Premium) uses your own private iCloud database. Read the Privacy Policy.
