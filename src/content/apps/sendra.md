## Thoughtful, when you remember to be

Write a message while you are thinking of someone, pick a contact, and set the date and time. Sendra reminds you at that moment so you can send it.

## Words when you are stuck

Dozens of warm templates cover birthdays, anniversaries, check-ins and thank-yous, and you can save your own.

## A monthly look back

Each month brings a recap with your message stats, top recipient, sending streak and a playful personality archetype to share as a card.
