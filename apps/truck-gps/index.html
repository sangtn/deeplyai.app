Truck GPS

Truck routes that show their work.

Requires iOS 18.6+Version 1.0, updated August 22, 2026

Get it free on the App Store

A route you can check

Truck GPS tells you which bridge it routed you around, what the clearance is and where that number came from. Routes are calculated against your truck’s height, width, length, weight, axle count and hazmat class, and cross-checked with the FHWA National Bridge Inventory, official US government bridge data.

It works where signal does not

The routing engine runs on your iPhone and map regions are stored on the phone, so a dead zone changes nothing.

Clear about what it knows

A badge shows whether you are on a Verified Truck-Safe Route from the on-device engine or an Enhanced Route using Apple’s live traffic, re-checked against your truck’s restrictions. If a region cannot be verified, the app stops and says so instead of guessing, and it never quietly downgrades you to a car route.

Paperwork included

Distance is recorded per jurisdiction as you drive and totalled by quarter, with a fuel log and a CSV you can hand to your accountant.

Nothing to sell

No account, no ad trackers and no analytics. Your position stays on the phone.
