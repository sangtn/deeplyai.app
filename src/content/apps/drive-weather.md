## The forecast that moves with you

A city forecast tells you about now. Drive Weather shows the conditions at each location at your expected arrival time there, answering three questions: what will I hit, when should I leave, and what is just ahead of me?

## Plan the departure

Drag the Departure Dial up to 7 days forward or back. The weather along your whole route recalculates for the hour your vehicle passes each point.

## Radar that is honest about itself

Radar draws on multiple sources, so coverage does not silently go stale mid-trip. Scrub recent frames with a clear timestamp on each one, and see plain labelling when short-term nowcasts are not available in your region.

## Warnings for what you drive

Set your vehicle type and hazard warnings adapt to it.
