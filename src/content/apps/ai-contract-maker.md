Create non-disclosure, freelance, service, contractor and consulting agreements, plus statements of work, from smart templates.

AI assistance can rewrite a clause professionally, simplify complex language and improve payment terms. Risk highlighting points to sections worth a second look.

Edit, review, sign and export to PDF. The app is offline-first and processes documents securely on your device.

AI Contract Maker is a drafting tool and does not provide legal advice. For anything important, ask a qualified lawyer to review the final document.
