## From photo to preview

Photograph a room or choose an image from your library, then tap a wall. AI detects and masks the surface, whether it is a wall, ceiling, door, trim or furniture.

## Decide with a real comparison

Choose from more than 200 curated colors or any custom color, adjust opacity and texture blend, and compare with a before-and-after slider. Share the result with family or your decorator.

## Private by design

Everything runs offline on your device. There is no account to create and nothing is uploaded to the cloud.
