## The globe

Thousands of tracked satellites orbit a photoreal Earth at their true positions, with day and night, clouds and city lights. Tap one to see a 3D model, its orbit path and live telemetry, or switch among Realistic, Night Lights, Vector and Minimal styles.

## Then go outside

Personalised pass predictions show rise, peak and set times and direction for where you are standing, so you can find the ISS, a Starlink train or Hubble yourself.

## Where the data comes from

Positions are calculated by a built-in SGP4 orbital engine, using live public data from CelesTrak, NOAA and The Space Devs.
