## Vietnamese timekeeping, wherever you check the date

Lịch Vạn Niên converts between the Vietnamese lunar calendar and Gregorian dates, and works across iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch and CarPlay.

## For the dates that matter to a family

- See favourable **Hoàng Đạo** hours and days, and avoid **Hắc Đạo** ones, when choosing a time for an important event.
- Plan weddings, death anniversaries and celebrations with lunar hour, day, month and year calculations, sorted into categories.
- Count the days between two dates, even when one is lunar and the other solar.

## Time for stillness

A built-in audio player offers a curated library of Buddhist sutras to support mindfulness.
