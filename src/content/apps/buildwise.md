## Enter dimensions the way trades do

BuildWise is built around fast feet-inch-fraction input. Calculate, convert and save results for construction, framing, roofing, stair layout and materials without breaking focus.

## Free and Pro

Core estimating tools are free. A BuildWise Pro subscription or one-time lifetime purchase adds:

- AI-powered estimates
- LiDAR room scanning
- PDF proposals
- Voice workflows

Prices are shown in the app before any purchase.
