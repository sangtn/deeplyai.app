Car.TV sample playlist

For App Review and anyone trying Car.TV. Every stream below is published by its owner for testing video players, or is a Blender open movie (CC BY).

Fastest way to test

  1. Open Car.TV and tap the blue + at the top of any screen.
  2. Tap Sample & free channels › Add sample playlist.
  3. Open Live TV and play Live Test Channel 1, or Library › Movies › Big Buck Bunny.

Or add it by link

In Car.TV: + › M3U / M3U8 URL, and paste:

https://deeplyai.app/review-playlist.m3u

This is a playlist file for a video player. Opened in a web browser it does not play — paste it into Car.TV instead.
