## Nothing assumed

SewingTut starts with your first stitch and builds up through structured learning paths. Guided lessons cover stitching, seams, hems, zippers and buttons.

## Learn by making

You practice on real items instead of drills, and the app explains how to read and follow a sewing pattern if you have never used one. Tracking your projects shows how far you have come, which helps on the days a seam goes wrong.
