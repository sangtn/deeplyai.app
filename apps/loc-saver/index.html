Loc Saver

A GPS toolkit for saving and replaying places.

Requires iOS 16.0+Version 1.1, updated August 17, 2026

Available on

  • iPhone
  • iPad
  • Mac
  • Apple Vision

Precise readings, ready to share

See latitude, longitude, altitude, accuracy, heading and speed in one place, then copy or share coordinates.

Simulation that stays inside the app

Set a static location, simulate a route with speed control or replay movement on a timeline to visualize a path before a real trip.

Loc Saver is not a system-wide fake GPS tool. Simulations happen only inside the app and do not change the location other apps see.

A record of where you have been

Save places with notes and tags and turn them into travel memories.
