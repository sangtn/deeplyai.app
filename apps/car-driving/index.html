On the road

Tools for people who spend real time behind the wheel, whether that means a daily commute, a long-haul truck, an electric car or a weekend away in an RV.

  • Car Play ConnectTurn your iPhone or iPad into a smart car dashboard.
  • CarWidgetDesign and rotate your own CarPlay widgets.
  • Loc SaverExplore, simulate and save locations.
  • Fuel FinderThe cheapest fuel near you, with widgets and CarPlay.
  • Boating NavigationPlan routes and track your boat, even offline.
  • Car Key ConnectTrack fuel costs, maintenance and vehicle expenses.
  • RoadCamA dual-camera dash cam with map and speed on one screen.
  • RV Camping PlannerA trip planner built for RVers and van-lifers.
  • Drive WeatherThe weather at each point of your route, when you reach it.
  • Truck GPSTruck routes that show which bridges they avoid and why.
  • EVPeekA second instrument cluster for your electric car.
  • GapMeterMeasure your following distance with the iPhone camera.
