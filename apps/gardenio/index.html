Gardenio

Organize, track and care for every plant.

Requires iOS 18.6+Version 1.1, updated August 17, 2026

Get it free on the App Store

One place for every plant

A balcony, a row of houseplants and a backyard vegetable patch can each be their own garden. Track as many plants as you have and follow each through its lifecycle.

Small daily tasks

Gardenio turns care into simple suggestions and watering reminders, adjusted for the weather. It works offline, and a garden journal keeps your notes on what worked last season.
