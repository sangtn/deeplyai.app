WindGlance

Wind speed, gust and direction in one glance.

Requires iOS 18.6+Version 1.0, updated July 16, 2026

Available on

  • iPhone
  • iPad
  • Apple Watch
  • Mac
  • Apple Vision
  • Widgets
  • Live Activities & Dynamic Island
  • StandBy

A compass that is actually about wind

The dial holds true north as you turn your device, while an arrow shows where the wind is blowing, colored by Beaufort force. Pick from more than 100 faces, from classic and nautical to aviation HSI and radar.

Wherever you glance

  • Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets in every size, each with its own spot and compass style.
  • A Wind Session Live Activity in the Dynamic Island.
  • StandBy, plus an Apple Watch app and complications that work on their own.

Inside the app you get current wind and gust, a 24-hour chart and a 5 or 10-day forecast.
