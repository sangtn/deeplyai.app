## Log it before you forget

Open the app and add an expense in seconds, with no sign-up. It works completely offline, which matters in a remote village or on a cruise ship. Attach a receipt photo, spread a cost over several days and sort by category.

## Know what is left

Set a total budget, a daily budget or both. A budget ring shows spending as it happens, along with your daily average and a warning before you overspend.

It works as well for a solo backpacker as for a honeymoon or a weekend with friends.
