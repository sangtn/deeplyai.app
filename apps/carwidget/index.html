CarWidget

Make your CarPlay screen yours.

3.2 out of 5 from 11 ratings (US App Store)Requires iOS 18.6+Version 10.2026.1, updated November 1, 2026

Get it free on the App Store

Widgets designed for a glance

CarWidget brings customisable widgets to CarPlay and your iPhone Home Screen. Start from one of 316 templates or design your own.

A library worth browsing

Templates cover clocks, calendars, weather, countdowns, neon, retro and racing styles, alongside seasonal and cute collections. Search the full library and save favourites. Many templates are animated inside the app.

Widget rotation

Cannot pick one? Put between 2 and 10 widgets on rotation and your car screen switches between them on its own.

  • Play in order or on shuffle, like a playlist.
  • Use automatic timing, or set an interval from 5 minutes to 1 hour.
  • Pin a design you love or skip ahead with a tap.
  • Rotation keeps working while your iPhone is locked.
