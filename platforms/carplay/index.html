CarPlay

A car screen should ask for as little attention as possible. These apps show what matters on the display you already glance at.

Truck GPS brings navigation to the dashboard, Fuel Finder shows the cheapest station, EVPeek adds a charging tab and CarWidget lets you design the screen.

Keep your attention on the road. Set up apps while parked.

  • Lịch Vạn NiênA Vietnamese lunar calendar across all your Apple devices.
  • CarWidgetDesign and rotate your own CarPlay widgets.
  • Weather WidgetsLive weather on your Home Screen, Lock Screen and CarPlay.
  • Fuel FinderThe cheapest fuel near you, with widgets and CarPlay.
  • DayliPlan your whole day on a simple grid.
  • RoadCamA dual-camera dash cam with map and speed on one screen.
  • Drive WeatherThe weather at each point of your route, when you reach it.
  • Truck GPSTruck routes that show which bridges they avoid and why.
  • EVPeekA second instrument cluster for your electric car.
