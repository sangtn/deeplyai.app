## Plan around the light

Solara gives sunrise and sunset predictions for where you are, along with daily, light-based recommendations. It suits photographers scheduling a shoot and anyone building a routine around daylight.

## Always a glance away

Widgets in multiple sizes track the day in real time and adapt between day and night states. A dedicated Apple Watch app gives quick updates on the go.
