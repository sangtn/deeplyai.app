StandBy

Turn a charging iPhone on its side and it becomes a small display. These apps have something worth showing there.

Sky Map has a red Nightstand mode, while Drive Weather works on a nightstand or a car mount.

  • DayliPlan your whole day on a simple grid.
  • WindGlanceWind direction across widgets and every Apple surface.
  • Sky MapPoint your phone up to identify stars and planets offline.
  • Drive WeatherThe weather at each point of your route, when you reach it.
