## The Lock Screen as a schedule board

Dately shows today’s events, to-dos and habits as your wallpaper. Use your favorite photo behind it and adjust font size, color and layout.

## Calendar that handles a crowded day

View daily, weekly or monthly. The timeline handles overlapping events so nothing gets lost.

## To-dos with priority

Add deadlines, optional due times and four priority levels, with color showing what is urgent. Habits live alongside, so a day’s plan stays in one app.
