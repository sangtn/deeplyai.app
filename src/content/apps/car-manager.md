## What your car really costs

Log each fill-up and see MPG or L/100km, cost per mile and monthly fuel spend. Add repairs, insurance, tolls and parking, and monthly charts show where the money goes. You can compare vehicles side by side.

## Stay ahead of service

Set reminders by mileage or date for oil changes, tire rotations and brake inspections, and record each visit with its cost and shop.

## Recall alerts

The app checks the official NHTSA database for open safety recalls on your vehicle. An AI assistant can answer questions such as what a warning light means, but serious faults still call for a mechanic.
