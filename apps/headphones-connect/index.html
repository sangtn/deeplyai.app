Speakers & Headphones Connect

An equalizer and widget for your audio gear.

Requires iOS 16.6+Version 1.1, updated May 5, 2026

Available on

  • iPhone
  • iPad
  • Mac
  • Apple Vision
  • Widgets

Control from the Home Screen

A customizable audio widget gives you playback, volume boost and EQ settings without opening the app.

Shape the sound

Adjust the equalizer, add bass and raise output for Bluetooth headphones and speakers. Smart sound adaption tunes audio for the speaker or headphone model you use.

Boosted volume can be hard on your ears, so turn it up gradually and keep listening sessions comfortable.
