Car.TV: frequently asked questions

Updated November 20, 2026

Straight answers, including the ones that are not what you hoped. If something here is wrong for your car, tell us at info@deeplyai.app.

The short version

  • On the car screen, video plays only while parked, and only in cars whose maker has turned on video in CarPlay (iOS 26.4 or later). In those cars you get sound only while you drive. In cars without video in CarPlay, Car.TV doesn’t appear on the car screen at all.
  • No car maker has publicly announced video in CarPlay yet (November 2026). Check your own car with Car check in the app.
  • No account, no ads, no tracking. App Privacy label: “Data Not Collected”.

In the car

Why does the car screen play only sound while I drive?

That is how CarPlay video is designed, by Apple and for safety. When the car is parked, a car that supports video in CarPlay shows the picture. As soon as the car moves, CarPlay takes the picture away and the sound keeps playing, with Now Playing and the steering-wheel buttons. The picture comes back when you park.

No app can change this, and Car.TV doesn’t try. Never watch video while driving.

Which cars show video in CarPlay?

Video in CarPlay needs two things: an iPhone with iOS 26.4 or later, and a car whose maker has turned the feature on for that model and its software.

As of November 2026 we know of no car maker that has publicly announced support, so we don’t list any car as supported. When a maker announces it, we will add it to our guide to video in CarPlay with a link to the announcement. Until then, the honest way to know is to check your own car.

How do I check whether my car can show video?

  1. Update your iPhone to iOS 26.4 or later.
  2. Install Car.TV and connect your iPhone to CarPlay once.
  3. On your iPhone, open Car.TV › Settings. Car check is at the top. If your car offers video to apps, Car.TV appears on the car screen and Car check shows that video plays there while parked (or that iOS needs an update first).
  4. If Car.TV doesn’t appear on the car screen at all, your car doesn’t offer video in CarPlay, or iOS is older than 26.4.

Car check is free and comes before any purchase. You can also ask your car maker or dealer whether your model and its software version support “video in CarPlay”; a head-unit software update may be needed.

My other car video app stopped showing a picture after iOS 27.2. Does Car.TV have the same problem?

iOS 27.2 (late October 2026) stopped the workarounds that some apps used to show video in any CarPlay car: screen mirroring, pretending to be an AirPlay or DLNA receiver, or drawing a web browser on the car screen. Many people now report only sound, or a message that the car doesn’t support video.

Car.TV doesn’t rely on any of these. It uses Apple’s official CarPlay video, so it shows video only where Apple allows it: in cars whose maker has turned video on, while parked. That is less than those apps promised, but it is the way Apple supports. More in our guide.

My car doesn’t show video. What can I still do?

  • Car.TV won’t be on the car screen. Apple hides CarPlay video apps in cars that don’t support video, so Car.TV can’t appear there, not even for sound.
  • Car Mode on iPhone. A big, simple player for a passenger, or for you while parked, with the phone in a mount. The iPhone’s sound plays through the car as usual.
  • Music on iPhone. Play your Music library from the iPhone; its sound plays through the car as usual.
  • iPad in the back seat. Car.TV runs on iPad too.
  • Downloads. Save films for the road before you leave, so you don’t depend on mobile data.
  • Cast to a TV when you arrive: AirPlay, Chromecast and Google TV, or a DLNA smart TV.

Can passengers watch while the car is moving?

Not on the car screen: CarPlay removes the picture while the car moves, for every app. A passenger can watch on their own iPhone or iPad with Car.TV. The driver must never watch.

Is the Browser on the car screen?

No. The Browser in Car.TV 1.1 is on iPhone and iPad only and never appears in CarPlay. It opens websites in tabs and can save music and video files that a page offers as a download to your library. It doesn’t save from streaming services.

What you can watch

Why can’t I watch Netflix, YouTube or Disney+ on the car screen?

These services protect their videos with DRM, and their terms don’t allow other apps to play, copy or mirror their streams. Car.TV respects that, so it doesn’t play them and won’t promise it. Apps that claimed to “mirror” them usually showed a black screen, and iOS 27.2 stopped most of them.

What works instead:

  • Play your own sources in Car.TV: an IPTV playlist, your Plex, Jellyfin or Emby server, or your own video files.
  • Passengers can use the service’s official app on their own iPhone or iPad.
  • At home or at your destination, cast from the official app to a TV.

Does Car.TV include or sell channels?

No. Car.TV is a player. It does not provide, host, sell or recommend any channels, films or series. You add your own sources and are responsible for having the right to watch them. If someone sells you “Car.TV channels”, that is not us.

To try the app, tap + › Sample playlist: four demo channels and six short films that we host ourselves, made from Pexels stock footage.

What can I play with Car.TV?

  • M3U and M3U8 playlists and Xtream Codes logins, with a TV guide (XMLTV / EPG).
  • Plex, Jellyfin, Emby and WebDAV servers.
  • Video files from the Files app, Photos and AirDrop.
  • Since version 1.1: a Music library, files sent from a computer with Wi-Fi Transfer, and files saved with the Browser.

Can I cast to a TV?

Yes. In the player, tap the cast button at the top right. Car.TV lists AirPlay devices, Chromecast and Google TV, and DLNA smart TVs on the same Wi-Fi. If none appear, check that the TV is on and on the same Wi-Fi, and that Local Network is allowed for Car.TV in the iPhone’s Settings.

Privacy and Premium

What data does Car.TV collect?

None. There is no account, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and the App Store privacy label reads “Data Not Collected”. Your playlists, servers and history stay on your devices; passwords stay in the iOS Keychain. Read the Privacy Policy.

What does Car.TV Premium add, and what does it cost?

Free: one playlist, portal or server, today’s TV guide, CarPlay, casting and Apple Watch. The sample playlist doesn’t count toward the one source. In 1.1, the Browser, Wi-Fi Transfer and Music are free.

Premium: unlimited sources, Plex and WebDAV servers, a 2-day guide, unlimited downloads, iCloud sync, parental lock and more. US prices: $2.99 a week, $7.99 a month, $29.99 a year, or $59.99 once for lifetime. The App Store shows the price in your country.

Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the period. Payment is charged to your Apple Account. Before you pay, run Car check so you know what your car will show.

How do I restore, cancel or get a refund for Premium?

  • Restore: Car.TV › Settings › Restore purchases, or Restore on the paywall.
  • Cancel: Settings › Manage subscription, or in your Apple Account settings, at least 24 hours before the period ends.
  • Refund: at reportaproblem.apple.com. Apple handles all payments.
