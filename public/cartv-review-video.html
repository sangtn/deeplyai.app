Northern Lights (test video)

A 20-second stock video for trying Play in car in Car.TV's Browser. It is footage from Pexels (Pexels License, free for commercial use), re-encoded and hosted by DeeplyAi.

Direct link to the MP4 file

  1. In Car.TV, open the Browser tab and go to this page.
  2. Tap Play in car (or Play in Car.TV when no car is connected).
  3. The video plays in Car.TV's player. With CarPlay connected and the car parked it shows on the car screen; while driving, CarPlay plays the sound only.
