## One tap to start the clock

Begin a session and your earnings update every second on screen, on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island.

## Made for people with several clients

Set an hourly rate per project. Each project can show earnings in its own currency, using an automatically fetched live rate or a fixed one you enter, with support for more than 32 currencies.

Contractors, hourly workers and small business owners can all see what the day has actually been worth.
