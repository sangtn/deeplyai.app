Dayli

Plan your whole day on a simple grid.

Requires iOS 18.0+Version 1.0, updated June 25, 2026

Available on

  • iPhone
  • iPad
  • Apple Watch
  • CarPlay
  • Mac
  • Apple Vision
  • Widgets
  • StandBy
  • iCloud sync

See the day as blocks

In place of long task lists, Dayli gives you a visual timeline grid. Drag to create a block, resize or move it, and use color and categories to build routines.

Then do the work

Pomodoro and countdown timers support deep work sessions, with productivity tracking afterwards.

Off the phone too

Your plan appears on interactive Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, StandBy, Apple Watch and CarPlay.
