## Your conversations, in numbers

Import a chat to see your most-used words, emojis and expressions, plus response times, active hours and frequency. Daily, weekly and monthly trends show how a conversation changes.

## Compare and reflect

See how patterns differ between contacts and think about your own communication style.

## Stays on your phone

All analysis happens on your device, and your data is never stored, shared or sold. Please analyze only chats you have the right to use, and remember that statistics cannot tell you what someone feels.
