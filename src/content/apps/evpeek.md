## Reads your car, not a server

- **Tesla:** connect over Bluetooth using your own phone key, with no Tesla account or password.
- **Hyundai, Kia and Genesis:** a Bluetooth OBD-II adapter reads the battery management system directly, including state of health, cell balance, module temperatures and the pack’s own charge reading.
- **Any other car:** EVPeek still shows speed and logs trips using your iPhone’s sensors, and tells you that is the source.

## Every number explains itself

Each reading carries its source and its age. Stale values dim, values too old to trust are hidden, and derived numbers show their working.

## Drive Mode

One large number on a true-black screen, designed against a 1.2-second glance budget. Tap to switch between speed, range, charge or kWh remaining.

## Delivered versus billed

EVPeek measures the energy that actually reached your battery, so you can compare it with what you were charged for.
