About

We have a passion for useful software.

We build products that are powerful and super easy to use for everyone.

What we make

DeeplyAi publishes 52 apps on the App Store, designed for iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch and CarPlay. Some help drivers, pilots and boaters get where they are going. Others live quietly on a Home Screen or Lock Screen, or help someone plan a day, a garden or a family occasion on the Vietnamese lunar calendar.

Where it fits the problem, we use AI and on-device processing. Several of our apps run their core features entirely on the phone, so people’s data stays with them.

Our shelves

  • On the road (12 apps): Dash cams, truck routing, fuel prices and CarPlay tools.
  • Weather & sky (6 apps): Forecast widgets, wind, sunsets, stars and satellites.
  • Watch & devices (4 apps): Apple Watch media, PDFs, headphones and call screening.
  • Work & planning (12 apps): Planners, time tracking, estimates, contracts and scripts.
  • Create & make (6 apps): Text on curves, paint previews, sewing and crochet.
  • Life & travel (12 apps): Lunar calendars, trip budgets, gardens and daily habits.

Where to find us

DeeplyAi is based in Mai Dich, Ha Noi, Vietnam. You can reach us at info@deeplyai.app.
