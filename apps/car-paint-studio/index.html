Car Paint Studio

See a repaint or wrap on your real car first.

Requires iOS 18.6+Version 1.1, updated August 17, 2026

Available on

  • iPhone
  • iPad
  • Mac
  • Apple Vision

Try the color before you pay for it

Start with one photo of your car. On-device AI detects each part, including body, hood, bumpers, grille, wheels, lights, glass and mirrors, so you can tap a part and choose its color.

Why the preview looks painted

Recoloring is physically based, so highlights, reflections and shading stay where they belong. Edges are pixel-accurate at glass, trim and panel gaps, and it works on white, black, silver or any other starting color. More than 200 real factory colors are available.

Take it to the shop

Compare before and after with a slider, then export images or a PDF spec sheet for your body shop.
