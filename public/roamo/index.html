Privacy promise

Your trips are nobody's business.

ROAMO has no servers that collect your data, no accounts and no trackers. It is built to need as little as possible.

App Store privacy label Data Not Collected

Read the full privacy policy →

  • No account

    Open the app and go. No sign-up, no email, no login.

  • No analytics, no ads

    No advertising, no analytics and no tracking SDKs of any kind.

  • Location stays on your phone

    Your GPS position is used on your device to guide you. It is never stored on any server.

  • Only what a request needs

    To find a place or a route, only the search text or the route's start and end points are sent to the map service (Geoapify) to answer that request.

Questions? Answered.

Is ROAMO free?
Yes. Block World and Doodle Notebook are free forever, with search and turn-by-turn navigation. ROAMO Pro unlocks the other 13 worlds.
Do I need an account?
No. There is no sign-up and no login. Purchases are handled by Apple through your App Store account.
Which devices are supported?
iPhone with iOS 17 or later.
Does ROAMO track me?
No. ROAMO has no ads, no analytics and no tracking SDKs, and its App Store privacy label is “Data Not Collected”.
Where does the map data come from?
Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors, tiles from OpenFreeMap / OpenMapTiles, search and routing powered by Geoapify, rendered with MapLibre.
Is ROAMO connected to any video game?
No. ROAMO is an independent app and is not affiliated with any game publisher. The worlds are original styles inspired by game genres.
