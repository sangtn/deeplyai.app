Live Activities & Dynamic Island

Some things unfold over minutes or hours. Live Activities keep them on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island while they happen.

Follow an ISS pass in Sky Map, a charging session in EVPeek, your earnings in Hours Tracker or the current script line in Teleprompter AI.

  • Teleprompter AIA speaking studio for creators, coaches and educators.
