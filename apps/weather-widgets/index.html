Weather Widgets

Live weather on your Home Screen, Lock Screen and CarPlay.

5.0 out of 5 from 3 ratings (US App Store)Requires iOS 17.6+Version 11.2026.1, updated November 2, 2026

Get it free on the App Store

Know what is outside without opening anything

Weather Widgets is powered by Apple WeatherKit and shows hyperlocal conditions where you already look. Choose from more than 50 styles in small, medium and large sizes.

Three places to see it

  • Home Screen: live temperature, conditions and hourly forecasts.
  • Lock Screen: circular, rectangular and inline widgets that fit without clutter.
  • CarPlay: live temperature and conditions on your car’s display, handy for commutes and road trips.

Kind to your battery

Refresh intervals adapt to the weather: every 15 minutes during rain, storms or alerts, and every 60 minutes when conditions are stable.

Weather follows your location automatically, and you can search any city worldwide, with recent searches kept close at hand.
