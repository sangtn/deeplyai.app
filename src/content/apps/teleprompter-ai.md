## It listens, so you do not tap

AI Voice Sync advances the script with each word and pauses when you pause. No foot pedal is needed, and you can look straight into the camera.

## Start from a blank page

Enter a topic, then choose a format (TikTok, YouTube, Podcast, Education or Business) and a tone. The script generator writes a full draft ready to deliver.

## Finish in one app

Record, then generate animated captions with word-by-word highlights, ready for TikTok, Reels and YouTube Shorts.
