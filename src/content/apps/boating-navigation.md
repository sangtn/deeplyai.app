## A route you can assess before leaving the dock

Each route gets a Safety Score. Depth-based routing colors sections as safe, caution or danger, and dangerous areas are blocked instead of merely flagged.

## Charts that do not need a signal

Download nautical charts once and use them offline, with depth contours, seabed data, buoys, beacons and hazard markers in day, night and satellite modes.

## On the water

Track position, heading and speed in real time without internet. An anchor alarm detects drift, and man-overboard navigation is available in an emergency.

Conditions at sea change quickly. Use this app alongside official charts, notices and proper seamanship.
