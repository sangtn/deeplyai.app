## When a conversation needs to outlast the chat

Some threads are worth keeping: a family exchange, an agreement with a landlord, a record you may need later. Text Export saves conversations from messaging apps on your iPhone into files you control.

## Formats and apps

Export to **PDF**, **CSV** or **plain text**. The app works with conversations from SMS and iMessage as well as WhatsApp, Messenger, Instagram DMs and Telegram.

Use a PDF when someone else has to read the conversation, and a CSV when you want to sort or search it in a spreadsheet.
