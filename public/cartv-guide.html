Connect to CarPlay

  1. Check your carCar.TV shows video on the car screen only in cars that support video in CarPlay, with iOS 26.4 or later. Other cars play the sound. Settings › Car check tells you what your car can do.
  2. Plug in, or go wirelessConnect your iPhone to the car's CarPlay USB port with a cable, or use wireless CarPlay if your car has it.
  3. Turn on CarPlay on iPhoneSettings › General › CarPlay › choose your car. Turn on Allow CarPlay While Locked.
  4. Find Car.TV on the car screenTap Car.TV on the CarPlay home screen. Not there? On iPhone: Settings › General › CarPlay › your car › Customize › Apps.
  5. Pick something to watchLive TV, My videos, Music and Playlists are on the car screen, as on your phone.
  6. Video when parkedWhen the car moves, CarPlay stops the picture and Car.TV keeps the sound — the phone shows "Car screen · sound only".
  7. Steering-wheel buttonsDuring live TV, next and previous change the channel. Turn this off in Settings › Playback.
  8. Passengers: Car ModeOn the phone, Car Mode gives big tiles and big buttons. Drivers, keep your eyes on the road.

Get started

  1. Welcome to Car.TVYour live TV, films, videos and music — on your iPhone, on a TV, and on the car screen when parked. Here's a 1-minute tour.
  2. Add anything hereAn IPTV playlist (M3U), an Xtream portal, a Jellyfin, Emby or Plex server, a cloud folder, or videos from Files and Photos.
  3. Search everythingChannels, films, series, your own videos and songs.
  4. SettingsYour sources, Premium, language, Car Mode, the parental lock — and this guide.
  5. HomePicks up where you left off: Continue watching, On now and New in your library.
  6. Live TVEvery channel, by country or with filters. Long-press a channel for its guide and Favorites.
  7. LibraryYours first: My videos, Music and Playlists play offline. Movies and Series come from your sources.
  8. SavedYour favorites and downloads, for when there's no signal.
  9. No playlist yet?Try the sample channels and open films to see how everything works.
  10. The playerOpens full screen. Swipe it down and it keeps playing in a small bar while you browse.
  11. Bring your filesSend videos and music from a computer with Wi-Fi Transfer, or save them from the web with the Browser.
  12. Using Car.TV in the car?See how to connect CarPlay, step by step. You can replay this tour any time in Settings › How to use Car.TV.

Questions

Why does the car screen play only sound while I drive?

That is how CarPlay video works, for safety: the picture shows only while the car is parked. When the car moves, CarPlay stops the picture and the sound keeps playing. No app can change this.

Car.TV isn't on my car screen. Why?

Car.TV appears only in cars that support video in CarPlay, with iOS 26.4 or later. Check Settings › General › CarPlay › your car › Customize › Apps, and see Car check in Car.TV's Settings.

Does Car.TV come with channels?

No. Car.TV plays what you add: an IPTV playlist or portal you have access to, your media server, or your own videos and music. Try the sample playlist to see how it works.

What plays without internet?

Everything in My videos and Music, your downloads, and playlists made of them. A playlist tells you when an item needs the internet.

How do I get my videos and music into Car.TV?

From Files or Photos with the + button, from a computer with Wi-Fi Transfer, by AirDrop or the Share button, or from the web with the Browser.

How do I make a playlist with a photo slideshow?

Library › Playlists › New, add songs, then Add a slideshow and pick photos. Play the playlist and the photos fill the screen while the songs play.

Do my playlists show on my other devices?

Yes, with Premium and iCloud sync turned on in Settings. Videos and songs stay on the device they were added to, so a playlist on another device lists what that device has.

What does Premium add, and how do I cancel?

Unlimited sources, Plex and WebDAV servers, the guide for the next 2 days, unlimited downloads, iCloud sync, the parental lock, and A/V sync with stats. Cancel any time in your Apple Account's subscriptions.

Does Car.TV collect my data?

No. There is no account, no ads and no tracking. Your sources, playlists and photos stay on your devices and, if you turn it on, in your own iCloud.
