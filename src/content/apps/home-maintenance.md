## Everything about the house, findable

Track appliances and systems, get reminders for maintenance deadlines, and keep receipts and manuals searchable. Warranty information comes with expiry alerts, so you claim before it lapses.

## One number for the state of things

The Home Health Score sums up your home’s condition.

## Ready when the power is out

An emergency center holds utility shutoffs and contacts, and the entire app works offline.

Every piece of data stays on your iPhone. There is no account and no server holding your home’s details.
