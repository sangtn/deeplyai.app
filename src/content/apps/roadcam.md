## Two cameras, one screen

RoadCam records the road through the rear camera and the cabin through the front camera at the same time, with the map, your speed and the speed limit on the recording screen. You can use it in portrait or in a landscape Split Dashboard layout.

## Built so footage survives

- Footage is saved every second, so a call, a crash or a dead battery costs you at most the last minute.
- Crash recovery restores footage after an unexpected shutdown.
- The G-sensor locks the clip around a collision so loop recording never overwrites it.
- Thermal Guardian steps quality down in the heat instead of letting recording die, and tells you what is happening.

## Hands-free when it counts

Save a moment with Siri or the Action Button. Traffic-Stop mode keeps recording after you park, so you do not have to touch your phone.

## Your footage stays yours

Recording happens entirely on your iPhone, with no cloud and no subscription required to keep what you have captured.
